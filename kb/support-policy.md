# Northwind Outfitters — Support Knowledge Base

Fictional demo store used by MemoDesk (ContextCon hackathon entry).
Policies below are the ground truth the triage agent retrieves from Meko.

## Refund policy
- Refunds are available within 30 days of delivery for unused items in original packaging.
- Damaged or defective items are refunded in full, including return shipping.
- Refunds over $200 must be escalated to a senior agent before approval.
- Duplicate charges are refunded immediately for the duplicated amount, no waiting period.
- Store credit is an accepted refund method when the customer prefers it.

## Duplicate charge
- A duplicate charge means the same order was charged twice on the same card.
- Action: verify the two charges, refund the duplicate immediately, and tell the customer the refund posts in 3-5 business days.
- Record the refund method used so the next agent handling this customer reuses it.

## Password reset
- Password reset links expire after 15 minutes.
- If a customer says the link does not work, first ask whether the link is older than 15 minutes, then send a fresh link.
- Never ask a customer for their password.

## Shipping delay
- Carrier backlog: standard deliveries in the northern region are currently delayed 3-5 days.
- If a package is stuck for more than 5 days, open a carrier trace and offer the customer a choice of waiting or a replacement shipment.
- Always quote the order number back to the customer.

## Delivery address change
- A delivery address can be changed free of charge before the order is dispatched.
- Once dispatched, the address cannot be changed; the parcel must be re-routed by the carrier for a fee.

## Escalation rules
- Refund over $200: senior agent.
- Suspected fraud or a chargeback threat: fraud team.
- Service outage affecting many customers: incident team, priority urgent.
